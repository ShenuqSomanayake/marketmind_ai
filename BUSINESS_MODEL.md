# MarketMind AI — Business model draft

This is a planning document, not a validated pricing or revenue forecast.

## Suggested freemium structure

| Plan | Indicative price (AUD/month) | Potential inclusions |
|---|---:|---|
| Free | $0 | Basic charts, watchlists, limited analysis |
| Pro | $19.99–$29.99 | Advanced research, screeners, portfolio analytics, backtesting |
| Premium | $49.99–$69.99 | Larger research allowance, advanced quantitative tools, prediction analytics |
| Professional | $99–$149 | Research workspace, exports, API allowance and team features |

Validate willingness to pay, support costs, AI inference costs and market-data licensing before setting prices.

## Potential revenue streams

- Individual subscriptions
- Business/API licensing
- White-label licensing
- Research workflow software for professional users
- Disclosed affiliate referrals where permitted and never allowed to influence analysis

## Illustrative subscription arithmetic

At A$24.99/month, 100 paying subscribers represent A$2,499 gross monthly recurring revenue before hosting, data, AI, payment, support, compliance and tax expenses. This is arithmetic, not a forecast.

## Compliance checkpoint — Australia

Before selling personalised or general financial product advice, obtain advice from an Australian financial-services lawyer/compliance specialist about whether an AFS licence or authorisation is required. A disclaimer alone does not determine the regulatory position. Review ASIC guidance: https://www.asic.gov.au/regulatory-resources/financial-services/giving-financial-product-advice/

Obtain appropriate market-data rights before redistributing quotes, derived data or reports commercially. Review privacy, consumer law, cybersecurity, tax and subscription cancellation obligations before public launch.
